Frequently asked questions

Before the first conversation.

What services does Tafolli & Co. offer?

We build websites, manage social media and support SEO and local business listings. Our services also include branding and print design, custom software, iOS and Android apps, cybersecurity, consulting and AI automation. Services can be commissioned individually.

Can you redesign an existing website?

Yes. We review content, structure, the domain and existing integrations. Important URLs and redirects are considered. Design, content, functions and future maintenance are set out in the proposal.

Do you provide ongoing social media and local listing support?

Yes. Editorial planning, posts, reels and business profile updates can be arranged as ongoing services. Channels, formats, approvals and scope are agreed first. Advertising media budgets are a separate cost item.

What does a project cost, and how reliable is the planner?

The planner uses editable example budgets and assumptions. It helps you prepare, but is neither a binding quote nor a promise of reach or customers. Actual costs follow the agreed scope of work.

Do you also develop software and AI assistants?

Yes. We plan custom business software, web and mobile applications and integrations. AI assistants and workflows are developed for specific tasks, with selected sources, checks and human approval.

How does working together begin?

Describe your goal or send your plan from the budget planner. We then clarify the starting point, scope, responsibilities and costs. Digital coordination can take place remotely; meetings and ongoing support are agreed for the project.

What should we help you solve?

Tell us about the project, your existing systems and your goal. Together, we will identify a sensible starting point.
